The home

Home & granny flat with sea views in Macleans

How often do you come across a home with separate accommodation below, panoramic sea views and private outdoor living in the exclusive Macleans zone at an affordable price range? Look no further, this home has it all, but only if you hurry.

Nestled behind a high fence with a sunny, private courtyard complete with gorgeous plantings, this home sits in a mature Mellons Bay cul-de-sac. Upstairs, the views from the open-plan lounge, dining and deck area are spectacular, right out to Waiheke Island. This living space is north-facing and sun-drenched with plenty of natural light. The ideal place to curl up and read a book or simply pour a glass of wine and soak up the views. With entertaining areas both to the front and rear of the home, there is always somewhere to relax, to entertain, to enjoy.

Two light-filled bedrooms offer a choice of accommodation and the character bathroom with original timber floorboards adds a quiet charm to the home. The kitchen also features the same rich timber floors, ample storage, lots of workable bench space and those stunning views. It will be hard not to become distracted while cooking the family meal! An enclosed carport with workshop space and off-street parking for two cars complete this level.

Downstairs there is the option of both internal and external access, offering flexibility as separate accommodation or part of the family home. The downstairs area includes a generous bedroom with walk-in robe, an open-plan lounge, dining and kitchenette, a bathroom and storage room with another sun-blessed deck embracing those views. All surrounded by easy-care, mature gardens. Whether you are accommodating extended family, welcoming homestay students or exploring additional rental income, the options are appealing.

In zone for Macleans College and conveniently located close to Howick Village, shops and public transport, this property is handy to everything. With beaches, parks and recreational facilities also nearby, this is an exceptional opportunity to secure a versatile home in a sought-after coastal location. This home will be popular. Call now!
