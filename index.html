FAQ

Questions we get asked

What is virtual renovation, and how is it different from staging?

Staging adds furniture to an empty room. Virtual renovation goes further: it can move or remove what is there, change floors, benchtops and wall colours, and show a new layout, all inside the 3D tour. The furniture comes from licensed 3D catalogues at true size. It is in development now, and you can ask for early access.

Is it honest to show a renovated home?

Only if it is clearly labelled, and ours always is. New Zealand's Fair Trading Act does not allow advertising that gives a misleading impression, so every renovated or staged view is marked as a concept and the real room is always one tap away. Photo finishing only corrects light and colour, and twilight shots are marked as simulated.

Do we have to take the photos or do the scan ourselves?

No. We visit once and capture everything: the 3D scan and the photos. You just tell us when the home is ready.

Does the 3D tour work on phones, Trade Me and Airbnb?

The tour runs in any web browser with nothing to install. You get a link to share on Trade Me, realestate.co.nz, social posts or in your Airbnb messages, plus photos and a floor plan ready to upload.

What does it cost?

We quote a fixed price per property after a short chat, so you know the full cost before anything starts. Your first sample is free.

Are the homes on this page real listings?

No. They are published 3D scans licensed for reuse (credited at the bottom of the page), used to show our work. For your property we scan the real thing.
