FAQ

Questions agents ask us

Do you change what the home actually looks like?

No. Photo finishing corrects light, colour and sharpness only. Nothing is added, moved or removed, every edited photo carries a label, and buyers can switch back to the original. Twilight images are always marked as simulated.

Do we have to take the photos or do the scan ourselves?

No. We visit the home once and capture everything: the 3D scan and the photos. You just let us know when the home is ready.

Does the 3D tour work on phones?

Yes. It runs in the web browser on phones, tablets and laptops with nothing to install. A light version appears first and full detail streams in as buyers move around.

Do we have to learn any software?

No. You get a link to the listing page and the files in the launch pack. The agent studio is there if you want to look at the numbers.

Can it go on Trade Me and realestate.co.nz?

We prepare the photos, listing copy and floor plan ready to upload, and a tour link you can share anywhere. Publishing straight into a portal depends on your agency's account access, so we set that up with you.

What does it cost?

We quote a fixed price per listing after a short chat, so you know the full cost before anything starts.

Are the homes on this page real listings?

No. They are published 3D scans licensed for reuse (credited at the bottom of the page), shown so you can try every tool. For your listings we scan your homes.
