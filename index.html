FAQ

Common questions

What is virtual renovation, and how is it different from staging?

Staging adds furniture to an empty room. Virtual renovation goes further: it can move or remove what is there, change floors, benchtops and wall colours, and show a new layout, all inside the 3D tour. The furniture comes from public-domain (CC0) 3D models at true size. Both staging and renovation are in development, and you can ask for early access.

Is it honest to show a renovated home?

Only if it is clearly labelled, and ours always is. New Zealand's Fair Trading Act does not allow advertising that gives a misleading impression, so every renovated or staged view is marked as a concept and the real room is always one tap away. Photo finishing only corrects light and colour.

Where does the furniture come from?

Every piece in our staging catalogue is a public-domain (CC0) 3D model from Poly Haven, so there is no copyright to clear and nothing to credit. Models are shown at their real size.

Do we have to take the photos or do the scan ourselves?

No. We visit once and capture everything: the 3D scan and the photos. You just tell us when the home is ready.

Does the 3D tour work on phones and in listings?

The tour runs in any web browser with nothing to install. You get a link to share in your listing text, social posts or Airbnb messages, plus photos and a floor plan ready to upload. Whether a portal can show the tour on its own page depends on that portal.

What does it cost?

It depends on the size of the home and what you need. Tell us about the property and we will give you a quote before anything starts.

Are the homes on this page real listings?

No. They are published 3D scans licensed for reuse, captured by other creators and credited at the bottom of the page. VYRA is new, so they show what the service looks like until our first New Zealand homes are done. For your property we scan the real home, and the level of detail can differ from these samples.
